## Source: Frank's 2009 charter-era brochure (Aug 25, 2026)

Frank sent `missAnnBrochure09.pdf` (a marketing brochure from her 2009–2013
operating-charter years) and said to use its photos and text for content,
noting **she is no longer an operating charter**. Three photos went straight
in (above); everything charter-specific was left out per the site's hard
rule — no charter/cruise/booking/rental content anywhere:

- Skipped entirely: the "Miss Ann Charters" branding, event/wedding/corporate
  marketing copy, the Gangplank Marina address and phone, and the sample
  charter itinerary.
- The 1920s–40s history (construction, maiden voyage, Sea Wolf, Aquamarine's
  WWII service, the Tides Inn restoration story) is well-documented history,
  not charter marketing, and was worked into `History.tsx`'s era text and the
  Vessel Particulars card.

**Resolution limit:** the brochure PDF has each page flattened to a single
~850×1100px JPEG, so extracted crops are soft once enlarged — acceptable for
the sepia 1920s–40s photos already in place (period-appropriate softness),
but **not usable for `hero.jpg`, `og.jpg`, or `restore.jpg`**, which need
real width. The brochure also has a few full-color, higher-impact shots
(a bow nameplate close-up, an aerial running shot, the wheelhouse, the main
salon) that would be excellent for those slots or for `/restore` — worth
asking Frank if he has the original photo files (not just the printed/PDF
brochure) at full resolution.

## Rights notes

- Photos from the original Miss Ann website or Frank directly are the
  preferred source — see above.
- Do **not** copy images from Hagley Museum, Virginia DHR, Clio, or press sites
  until written permission is confirmed (rights pending). The site links to those
  collections instead.
- U.S. Navy photographs (DANFS / NavSource originals) are public domain and safe
  to use with a credit line.
